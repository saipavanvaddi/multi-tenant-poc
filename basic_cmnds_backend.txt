above is for 


Put it near ALLOWED_HOSTS.

Why?

Later, Next.js will call Django using:
http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/v1/products/


But the actual visitor might be visiting:
ananya.localhost:3000


Next.js will forward that hostname using:
X-Forwarded-Host: ananya.localhost:3000


Django will use the forwarded hostname to identify the correct shop.

Django still validates the hostname against ALLOWED_HOSTS. In production, this header must only be trusted from your controlled proxy, not arbitrary clients.
